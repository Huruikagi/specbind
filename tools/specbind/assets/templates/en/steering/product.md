# Product

<!-- specbind:instruction maintain
Describe what this project is for and who it serves, so a newcomer can tell
whether a proposed change belongs here at all.

Record the purpose and the constraints it puts on the work. Leave out feature
inventories: a list that has to be updated whenever a feature ships is a list
nobody will keep current.

Say what the project deliberately does not do when that boundary has already
turned a request away. A stated non-goal saves the argument next time.
-->

## Purpose

<!-- specbind:instruction maintain
State the long-lived problem the project solves and why it exists. Do not list
current features or the short-term roadmap.
-->

## Who it serves

<!-- specbind:instruction maintain
Identify the people or operators the project primarily serves and the needs

-->

## Core capabilities
